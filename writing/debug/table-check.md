# Table Check Easter Egg

Quips for the rare table-check easter egg (scaffolded in debug rail).
Sections: `## checking`, `## accept`, `## reject`. Supports `{gl:base|glitch}`.

## checking
- Hold on. Let me {gl:check|re-read} the table.
- Wait—is that actually in here?
- Hang on, checking the back pages.
- One sec. Dusting off the index.
- Let me {gl:verify|squint at} that spelling.
- Checking the {gl:deep|forbidden} drawer.
- Hold everything. Reading the fine print.
- Give me a cycle. Looking it up.

## accept

- Oh—{gl:good catch|didn’t think anyone knew that}.
- Found it. Way at the bottom.
- Fair enough. Pushing frames.
- Okay, okay, you win this one.
- Barely legible, completely legal.

- Nope. Checked twice. Not in there.
- Nice try. {gl:I almost believed you.|Still no.}
- Not on the list—and I looked.
- Pretty sure you {gl:made that up|hallucinated that}.
- Zero hits. Try another angle.
- I squinted as hard as I could. No.
