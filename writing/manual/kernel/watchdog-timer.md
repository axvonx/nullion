# The Watchdog

id: watchdog-timer · tag: WDT(0) · kind: prop · rarity: rare · status: approved · page: 17

Patiently counts down in the dark — if software forgets to pet it, it barks and resets the machine.

> Hardware watchdog timers recover frozen systems automatically by triggering a hard reset on timeout.
